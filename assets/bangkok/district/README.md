# Bangkok street assets

These copies have embedded textures and simplified geometry. The source models are stored separately.

- `neo-storefront.glb` uses **Neo Bangkok** by daydev. The rain planes were removed. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/neo-bangkok-dc8b51521b1c4786aed8635c8e7b7061)
- `corner-shops.glb` uses storefronts from **1DAE02 Cityscene Bangkok Thailand** by NicoFoets. The game applies a plaster texture to the exposed side walls. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1dae02-cityscene-bangkok-thailand-6df74485e8644bcc85eddb546996383b)
- `moonlight.glb` retains the car, deck graphics, and lighting geometry for future use from **K/DA: Evelynn Dance Stage MoonLight Edition** by AnixMoon. The studio backdrop and reflection geometry were removed. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/kda-evelynn-dance-stage-moonlight-edition-404a94a1f65a4379a82db0c3382ffdd9)

These three source models are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The other street models use the supplied `city.glb`, `Cityscene Bangkok.glb`, and `tuktuk1.glb`.

The night market uses generated artwork in `night-surfaces.webp`, `night-shophouses.webp`, `lisa-theatre.webp`, and `jennie-theatre.webp`. The street maps the images over separate awnings, walls, windows, and venue entrances. `canal-houses.glb` contains selected architecture from the supplied **Bangkok - City Scene** model.

The distant city also uses **Cyberpunk City - #1** by born2012, with remapped, textured building façades and the street-level meshes removed. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cyberpunk-city-1-14d2eaa145ee42938e004115871adf6c) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
